Brand Identity & UI/UX

Driving Global Trust:
USA to UAE Logistics

A massive end-to-end design undertaking for a premium car shipping company specializing in importing vehicles from the United States to the United Arab Emirates.

I led the comprehensive visual overhaul—starting from the foundational brand logo, expanding into a full digital ecosystem (Website, App, System Dashboard), and scaling all the way up to physical environmental designs like building signage and highway billboards.

  • Brand Identity & Print Materials
  • Digital Ecosystem (Web, App, System)
  • OOH Marketing & Environmental Design

1. Core Brand Identity

Establishing trust and speed. The logo and print materials were designed to evoke a sense of premium logistics, utilizing sharp typography and high-end print finishes.

Logo & Typography

Conceptualized a modern emblem combining elements of motion, maritime shipping, and automotive luxury. Selected a robust sans-serif typeface to ensure high legibility across all mediums.

Corporate Stationery

Designed business cards, letterheads, and official customs document templates. Ensured the brand felt authoritative and reliable for international B2B and B2C clients.

2. Digital Ecosystem

Creating a seamless user experience across multiple touchpoints: from customer acquisition to shipment tracking and internal logistics management.

Public Website UI/UX

shipping-uae.com

Designed a conversion-optimized landing page featuring an instant quote calculator, robust service descriptions, and trust-building testimonials. Focused on a dark, premium aesthetic to match luxury car imports.

Client Tracking App

Mobile Interface Sequence

UI/UX design for the customer-facing mobile application, allowing users to track their vehicle's journey across the ocean with real-time GPS updates, push notifications, and digital document uploads. Expanded to a clean, landscape layout showing screen flow sequences.

3. Social Media Content

Designed an ongoing grid strategy to showcase successful shipments, educate clients on USA auction processes, and maintain a high-end luxury aesthetic.

4. Environmental & OOH

Translating the digital brand into massive physical spaces. Designing for real-world impact across corporate headquarters and highway advertising.

HQ Building Branding

Designed the exterior signage, window frosting patterns, and interior reception mockups for the main office in Dubai. Ensured maximum visibility and brand prestige for visiting clients.

OOH Billboards

Created high-impact, minimalist billboard designs aimed at high-net-worth individuals importing classic and exotic cars from the US. Optimized for fast readability at highway speeds.
