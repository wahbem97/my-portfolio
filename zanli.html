Market Entry & Digital Strategy

Expanding Horizons:
Luxury Furniture to KSA

A comprehensive strategic and operational campaign to promote and sell premium furniture in the Saudi Arabian market.

This project encompassed end-to-end execution: from defining the initial market entry strategy to hands-on team training, creative direction for photo shoots, and overseeing the localized e-commerce experience.

  • General Market Strategy & Positioning
  • Personnel Training & Content Production
  • E-Commerce Website Supervision

Project Execution Phases

A step-by-step breakdown of how we launched and scaled the furniture brand's presence in Saudi Arabia.

1

Setting the General Strategy

Conducted market research to define the target demographics in Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam. Established competitive pricing strategies, identified cultural nuances for marketing, and mapped out a high-value product positioning plan specifically tailored for Saudi consumers.

  • Market Mapping & Research
  • Brand Positioning
  • Cultural Adaptation
2

Training Personnel on Social Content

Developed and led hands-on workshops for the internal marketing and sales teams. Trained them on how to independently style furniture, utilize proper interior lighting techniques, and script engaging short-form video content to capture audience attention.

  • Content Creation Workshops
  • Lighting & Styling Best Practices
  • Short-form Video Scripting
3

Shooting & Publishing Content

Actively participated in on-site photo and video shoots to ensure the strategy translated into visual reality. Directed the creative vision, oversaw prop styling, and managed the scheduling and publishing of social media content across target platforms.

  • On-site Creative Direction
  • Prop Styling & Set Management
  • Multi-platform Publishing

Supervision of the Website

Supervised the e-commerce platform's adaptation for the Saudi market. This included ensuring flawless Arabic localization (RTL layout), overseeing the integration of local payment gateways (like Mada), and optimizing the UX for mobile shoppers.

Social Media Designs

Writing content and supervising the visual design for Instagram, and TikTok to engage the Saudi and arabic audience and drive the sales.
