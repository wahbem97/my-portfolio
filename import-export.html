Project

Bridging Markets:
China to Saudi Arabia

A comprehensive, multi-platform visual campaign designed to connect Chinese manufacturers with the Saudi Arabian market. My role encompassed the entire lifecycle of the campaign, from initial concept to final delivery.

  • Strategic Direction Set the overarching marketing strategy to bridge cultural and business gaps between the two regions.
  • Content Creation & Scripting Wrote engaging scripts and planned content tailored for B2B audiences and general consumers.
  • On-Ground Production in China Traveled to Chinese factories to shoot high-quality footage. Produced videos focused on three pillars: raising corporate credibility, increasing brand exposure, and driving direct sales.
