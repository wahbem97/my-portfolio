Scripting & Direction

Workflow & Animation Supervision

I bridge the gap between initial concept and final visual execution by handling both the scriptwriting and animation supervision for my projects. I begin the workflow by drafting the script, creating a solid foundational blueprint that establishes the narrative core, dialogue, and pacing. Once the story is locked in, I step into a supervisory role to oversee the motion graphics and animation process. By actively guiding the timing, transitions, and overall mechanics of movement, I maintain strict quality control and ensure that the final on-screen visuals perfectly align with the script's original creative intent.
